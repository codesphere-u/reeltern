              Reversible DAG · Segment Cache
              
Never Lose an Edit or Re-Encode Unchanged Clips

              
Every change creates a child revision linked by SHA-256 parent ID. Rendered video segments cache by normalized source hash, trim, speed, grade, motion, and transition — turning re-renders around in sub-second hits.

              
v1 · suggest_edit()         ──► 2 segments encoded · 0.56s
v2 · split_clip(0, offset=1.2) ──► clip 1 cached!     · 0.34s
v3 · configure_audio_mix(duck=true) ──► receipt SHA-256 verified
