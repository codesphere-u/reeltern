Everything Reeltern ships

Shared deterministic Python core across the Desktop GUI, CLI, and 31-tool MCP stdio server — zero cloud uploads, zero telemetry.

Commands & CLI

reeltern compose

Build a complete multi-clip plan in one command using AI Director styles, music ducking, titles, preview & audit.

reeltern inspect

Extract PNG storyboard contact sheets, PyAV luma/contrast telemetry, scene cuts, and energy peaks.

reeltern audit <job_id>

Run EBU R128 loudness, true-peak headroom, and black-frame checks on rendered MP4 exports.

reeltern studio / gui

Open the desktop NLE workstation with live PyAV monitor preview (10 grades, blur_fill, guides), multi-track V1/T1/A1 timeline, [Mark In/Out], Load Demo, Connect AI & inline Audit QA.

reeltern card & transcript

Generate chapter slates, align supplied SRT cues, or draft bounded edits with an optional local Ollama model.

Video & Audio Engine

blur_fill Halo Framing

Turn landscape clips into 9:16 vertical reels over a Gaussian-blurred background without losing subject edges.

10 Color Grades & Ken Burns

Calibrated looks (teal_orange, cinematic, vivid, noir) plus smooth zoom_in, zoom_out, and pan motion.

Kinetic ASS Captions

Render high-contrast phrase word highlights and subtle progress bars from supplied transcripts — never fabricated speech.

Sidechain Music Ducking

Speech cleanup EQ (highpass=f=85) automatically attenuates music stems under voice and normalizes to -16 LUFS.

Visual PNG Inspection

Reversible DAG & Segment Cache

Sandboxed Media Root
